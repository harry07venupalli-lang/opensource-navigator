# Product roadmap

## Stage 1 — Current MVP

Public repository → evidence-led issue recommendations → a practical contribution path. The free core includes repository analysis, skill matching, likely files/tests, contribution rules, activity signals, and observable blockers.

## Stage 2 — Validation

Launch to real users and learn which repositories they analyze, issues they select, where they stop, whether they return, and whether they ask for deeper preparation. The included analytics interface is intentionally no-op outside development until a privacy-reviewed provider is chosen.

## Stage 3 — Deep analysis

Build the single highest-demand capability first: richer issue-to-codebase context, readiness analysis, edge-case/test preparation, or contributor roadmaps. It must remain evidence-led and never fabricate codebase understanding.

## Stage 4 — Monetization

Only after repeat use and explicit deep-analysis demand: add authentication if genuinely needed, privacy-conscious usage tracking, an entitlement layer, and payments. Do not add artificial restrictions to the useful free core.

## Stage 5 — Expansion

Potential contributor progression, learning paths, saved projects, private repositories through user-authorized access, and team workflows.
